# BK21 FOUR official-site review: AI/security scope

## Reusable finding

For venue-qualified AI/security paper work, treat three layers separately:

1. **Current program context:** inspect the official BK21 FOUR site for current duration, operating rules, evaluation direction, and its official education/research-group catalog.
2. **Field scope:** retain only entries explicitly relevant to AI, cybersecurity, or an explicitly requested intersection. Do not generalize from unrelated listed disciplines.
3. **Venue baseline:** use a dated public venue list or an institutionally hosted 4th-stage list copy only as a candidate gate. It does not prove a current universal policy or paper quality.

## Source pattern observed

- The official `자료실` can be current for management manuals and evaluations even when it does not publish a current field-specific CS venue list.
- The official education/research-group catalog can validate that AI and cybersecurity are represented as current supported areas, but it is not itself a venue eligibility list.
- A quality-oriented program evaluation means the review must still inspect paper-level method, evidence, limitations, and reproducibility.

## Reporting language

Use:

> "AI/security scope was confirmed from the current program catalog. The dated venue list is used as a public candidate baseline; paper quality was assessed from official publication records and full-text evidence."

Avoid:

> "The current BK21 site declares this venue eligible, therefore this paper is good."

## Minimum evidence to retain

- Central official program/operations URL and access date.
- AI and cybersecurity catalog URLs or filter results.
- Venue-list issuer, edition/effective date, source URL, and whether it is a central original or institutional reference copy.
- Candidate-level official proceedings/DOI evidence.
- Paper-level evidence ledger and limitations.
